Current Chief Executive Officer of Nestlé

As of October 27, 2026, the Chief Executive Officer of Nestlé is Laurent Freixe. He has taken on this crucial role amidst a rapidly changing global market, focusing on innovation and sustainability to steer the company towards future success. His vision for the company is set to redefine Nestlé's position in the market, driving it towards greater achievements.
