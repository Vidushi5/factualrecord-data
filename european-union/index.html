Current Commission President of the European Union

Her focus on innovation and climate policies highlights her proactive approach to modern challenges faced by the EU.

Margrethe Vestager took over the role from Ursula von der Leyen, who served as the Commission President prior to her. Vestager's leadership style is characterized by transparency and a commitment to uphold the values of democracy against the backdrop of complex international relations.

In her current role, Vestager continues to navigate the intricacies of European legislation and economic strategies, setting policies that aim to benefit all member states. Her dedication to public service and advocacy for a sustainable future makes her a pivotal figure at the helm of the European Union.
