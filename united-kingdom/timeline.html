LEADERSHIP TRANSITION July 4 – July 5, 2024

2024 General Election & Transfer of Power

Following parliamentary elections on July 4, outgoing Prime Minister Rishi Sunak tendered his resignation at Buckingham Palace on the morning of July 5. King Charles III subsequently invited Keir Starmer to form a government, formalizing the transfer of executive authority.

  • Trigger: UK General Election
  • Result: Labour Majority (411 seats)
