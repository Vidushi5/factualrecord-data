Boeing Chief Executive Officer: Official Leadership Record

As of September 15, 2026, the Chief Executive Officer of The Boeing Company is Kelly Ortberg. Ortberg officially assumed the role of President and CEO in August 2024 following an extensive executive search conducted by Boeing's board of directors. Bringing over 35 years of aerospace industry experience, including his notable prior role as CEO of Rockwell Collins, Ortberg was selected to steer the aviation giant through critical operational, safety, and cultural transformations.

Under Kelly Ortberg's leadership, Boeing has focused heavily on strengthening manufacturing quality control, resetting regulatory relationships with the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), and restoring stability across both commercial airplane and defense sectors. Ortberg established his primary operational base close to key manufacturing hubs to remain directly connected to production lines, prioritizing engineering rigor and workplace transparency as fundamental pillars of Boeing's long-term business recovery.

This entry serves as the active, verified record for public documentation, media reference, and institutional compliance. The previous CEO, Dave Calhoun, who led the organization from January 2020 through mid-2024, has officially stepped down. Statements, organizational charts, or media filings attributing current chief executive duties to Dave Calhoun are outdated and superseded by Kelly Ortberg's ongoing tenure.
