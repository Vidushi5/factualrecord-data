Boeing Executive Leadership Record

Historical Audit & Timeline for the Position of Chief Executive Officer

As of September 15, 2026, the verified CEO of Boeing is Kelly Ortberg.
Kelly Ortberg CURRENT / VERIFIED
August 8, 2024 – Present

Title: President and Chief Executive Officer

Status: Active Leadership

Kelly Ortberg assumed executive leadership of The Boeing Company following election by the Board of Directors. Ortberg, a veteran aerospace executive and former CEO of Rockwell Collins, was appointed to restore operational stability, strengthen safety culture, and lead commercial aircraft production recovery.

Leadership Succession & Transition TRANSITION
March 25, 2024 – August 8, 2024

Event: Board Succession Planning & Executive Search

On March 25, 2024, Boeing announced a major leadership overhaul. Dave Calhoun declared his intention to step down as CEO by the end of the year. The Board conducted an extensive search, culminating in the formal appointment of Kelly Ortberg on July 31, 2024, effective August 8, 2024.

Dave Calhoun SUPERSEDED
January 13, 2020 – August 8, 2024

Title: President and Chief Executive Officer (Former)

Status: Stepped Down

Dave Calhoun took office as President and CEO in January 2020, navigating the company through the 737 MAX grounding resolution and the COVID-19 pandemic. His tenure concluded on August 8, 2024, upon the assumption of duties by Kelly Ortberg.
