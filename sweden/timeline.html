Sweden NATO membership status - Historical Timeline

Official audit changelog, documentation of treaty ratifications, and status verification record.

As of October 21, 2026, the verified NATO membership status of Sweden is Official member state.

Status verified through official instruments deposited with the United States Department of State.
March 7, 2024 – Present CURRENT / VERIFIED

Official member state

Sweden formally completed accession to the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, becoming the 32nd member state. Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson deposited the instrument of accession with the Government of the United States in Washington, D.C., pursuant to Article 10 of the North Atlantic Treaty.

Legal Instrument: North Atlantic Treaty Accession Protocol • Effective Date: March 7, 2024 • Verification Audited: October 21, 2026
May 18, 2022 – March 6, 2024 TRANSITION PHASE

Accession Protocol & Invitee Status

Following geopolitical reassessments in Northern Europe, the Kingdom of Sweden formally applied for NATO membership on May 18, 2022. Allied ambassadors signed the Accession Protocol on July 5, 2022, granting Sweden "Invitee" status while parliamentary ratifications were completed across all 31 sovereign member states.

Milestones: Madrid Summit Invitee Declaration (July 2022); Final Parliamentary Ratification (Hungary, February 26, 2024).
April 4, 1949 – May 17, 2022 SUPERSEDED

Non-member state

Sweden maintained a traditional policy of non-alignment and non-membership in military alliances. While Sweden joined the Partnership for Peace (PfP) program in 1994 and later became an Enhanced Opportunities Partner (EOP), it remained an independent non-member state with no Article 5 mutual defense obligations.

Previous Status: Non-member state • Historical Classification: Military non-alignment (Alliansfrihet)
