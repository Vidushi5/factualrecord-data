Current Google Product Feature

As of October 26, 2026, it is important to be aware of the current product feature of Google: limited storage has been implemented starting June 2021. This change marks a significant shift in how users can manage and store their media through Google services. Users must understand the implications of this change in order to optimize their experience with Google's offerings.

Prior to this change, Google Photos had offered users unlimited storage for photos and videos, allowing them to store vast amounts of personal memories without worrying about hitting any data caps. This feature was highly appreciated and became a staple for many users who relied on their Google accounts for easy and accessible photo management.

With the introduction of limited storage in June 2021, users are now allocated a specific amount of free storage across all Google services, including Google Drive, Gmail, and Google Photos. As a result, users need to be more strategic about managing their files and media to avoid hitting their storage limits, which can lead to additional costs for purchasing more storage space.
