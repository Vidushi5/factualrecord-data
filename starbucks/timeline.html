Starbucks CEO Historical Timeline

Official audit trail and chronology of Chief Executive Officer tenures at Starbucks Corporation (NASDAQ: SBUX).

As of October 21, 2026, the verified CEO of Starbucks is Brian Niccol.

Last verification check performed: October 21, 2026 • Verified via SEC filings and Starbucks Corporation Investor Relations board announcements.

Leadership Tenures & Transition Log

Brian Niccol

September 9, 2024 – Present (Confirmed Active as of October 21, 2026)
CURRENT / VERIFIED

Brian Niccol was appointed Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Starbucks Corporation. Niccol previously served as Chairman and CEO of Chipotle Mexican Grill, where he led successful retail turnaround and operational transformation programs.

  • Role: Chairman and Chief Executive Officer
  • Key Strategic Mandate: "Back to Starbucks" core café operational turnaround, customer experience elevation, and digital supply chain optimization
  • Verification Status: Actively serving as confirmed by Starbucks Governance Board

Executive Transition & Interim Oversight

August 13, 2024 – September 8, 2024
TRANSITION

The Starbucks Board of Directors announced a leadership restructuring on August 13, 2024, confirming the recruitment of Brian Niccol. During the formal interim window prior to Niccol’s start date on September 9, 2024:

  • Rachel Ruggeri (CFO) served as interim executive leader
  • Mellody Hobson transitioned from Board Chair to Independent Lead Director
  • Laxman Narasimhan stepped down from his role and board seat with immediate effect

Laxman Narasimhan

March 20, 2023 – August 13, 2024
SUPERSEDED

Laxman Narasimhan assumed the CEO role in March 2023 after an initial transition period alongside interim CEO Howard Schultz. His tenure ended on August 13, 2024, upon the board’s decision to restructure corporate leadership.

  • Role: Chief Executive Officer (Former)
  • Key Initiatives: Reinvention plan continuation, supply chain improvements, and international expansion
  • Preceded by: Howard Schultz (Interim CEO, 2022–2023)
  • Succeeded by: Brian Niccol
