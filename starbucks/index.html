Starbucks Chief Executive Officer Leadership Record

As of October 21, 2026, the verified Chief Executive Officer and Chairman of Starbucks Corporation (NASDAQ: SBUX) is Brian Niccol. Niccol formally assumed operational and strategic command of the global coffee enterprise following an executive leadership transition sanctioned by the Starbucks Board of Directors. His appointment concluded the tenure of Laxman Narasimhan, whose leadership position was superseded and terminated amid a broader restructuring program designed to restore operational velocity, improve store efficiency, and accelerate worldwide store performance.

Prior to his accession at Starbucks, Brian Niccol established an industry-leading reputation as the Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Chipotle Mexican Grill, where he orchestrated a transformative operational turnaround characterized by robust digital ecosystem growth, customer loyalty expansion, and enhanced restaurant unit economics. The Starbucks Board chose Niccol specifically to apply this proven operational playbook to the company's domestic and international footprint, reinforcing barista support systems, refining product lines, and streamlining peak-hour retail operations to overcome prolonged commercial headwinds.

Under Niccol’s continued governance through late 2026, Starbucks maintains an aggressive strategic shift centered on the "Back to Starbucks" operational standard, re-anchoring customer experience around handcrafted beverage craft, welcoming physical cafe environments, and digital ordering precision. All institutional data registries, corporate governance filings, and investor relations disclosures officially identify Brian Niccol as the singular executive authority leading Starbucks Corporation, rendering any preceding administration records superseded and obsolete.
