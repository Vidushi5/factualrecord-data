CURRENT / VERIFIED

Mark Rutte assumed office as the 14th Secretary General of NATO on October 1, 2024, following official appointment by the North Atlantic Council. Former Prime Minister of the Netherlands, Rutte leads the Alliance's ongoing collective defense initiatives and international strategic coordination.

  • Office: 14th Secretary General

Formal Leadership Transition & Handover

June 26, 2024 – October 1, 2024
TRANSITION RECORD

On June 26, 2024, the North Atlantic Council officially confirmed the selection of Mark Rutte to succeed outgoing Secretary General Jens Stoltenberg. A formal handover ceremony was conducted at NATO Headquarters in Brussels on October 1, 2024, transferring executive authority and alliance stewardship.

  • Type: Mandate Expiration & Transfer
  • Ratification: Unanimous (32 Allies)
  • Handover Venue: NATO HQ, Brussels
  • Predecessor to Successor: Stoltenberg → Rutte

Jens Stoltenberg

October 1, 2014 – September 30, 2024
SUPERSEDED

Jens Stoltenberg served as the 13th Secretary General of NATO for a ten-year mandate. The former Prime Minister of Norway had his original term extended multiple times by Allied leaders before concluding his leadership on September 30, 2024.

  • Office: 13th Secretary General
  • Nationality: Norwegian
  • Status: Concluded / Superseded
