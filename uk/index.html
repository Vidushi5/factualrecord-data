As of October 26, 2026, the current Prime Minister of the United Kingdom is Rishi Sunak. He has been in office since October 25, 2022, following the resignation of his predecessor. Sunak, a member of the Conservative Party, has played a significant role in shaping the country's response to various challenges, including economic reforms and the ongoing impact of global events on the UK.

Previously, Liz Truss was the Prime Minister; however, she served a notably short term and stepped down after just a few weeks. Her premiership was marked by controversial economic policies, which led to widespread criticism and instability within the government. Rishi Sunak's ascension to leadership was viewed as a stabilizing choice by many within the party and the electorate.

Sunak's leadership style emphasizes fiscal responsibility and sustainable growth, aiming to restore public confidence in the government's ability to manage the economy. He has also focused on unity and reconciliation within the Conservative Party to ensure a cohesive approach to governance moving forward.
