Philip Moyer

CURRENT / VERIFIED
April 2024 – Present (Active October 21, 2026)

Philip Moyer was formally appointed Chief Executive Officer and member of the Board of Directors, succeeding the interim leadership structure. Under his leadership, Vimeo has prioritized AI-powered video workflows, enterprise video communications, and sustainable platform profitability.

Verification Details: Full executive powers confirmed via corporate disclosures and verified as of October 21, 2026.

Executive Search & Interim Leadership (Adam Gross)

TRANSITION PERIOD
August 2023 – April 2024

Following Anjali Sud's departure, Board member Adam Gross stepped in as interim CEO while the Board of Directors conducted an executive search for permanent leadership.

This interim governance concluded with the recruitment and official appointment of Philip Moyer in April 2024.

Anjali Sud

SUPERSEDED
July 2017 – August 2023

Anjali Sud served as CEO for over six years, pivoting Vimeo from an original content streaming service to a cloud-based B2B software and video tools provider, which culminated in Vimeo's public spin-off from IAC in May 2021.

Sud stepped down in August 2023 to pursue another executive opportunity at Tubi, concluding her tenure at Vimeo.
