Vimeo CEO - Historical Timeline

Official verified leadership ledger • Archive Reference: VIM-EXEC-TL

As of October 21, 2026, the verified CEO of Vimeo is Philip Moyer.

April 8, 2024 – Present CURRENT / VERIFIED

Philip Moyer

Chief Executive Officer

Vimeo's Board of Directors appointed Philip Moyer as Chief Executive Officer, effective April 8, 2024. Moyer joined the company after serving as Vice President of Strategic Industries at Google Cloud. He continues to actively hold the office of Chief Executive Officer.

August 31, 2023 – April 8, 2024 TRANSITION

Adam Gross

Interim Chief Executive Officer

Following Anjali Sud's departure, board member Adam Gross assumed leadership as Interim CEO to manage operational continuity, governance, and the executive transition while the board conducted an executive search.

July 2017 – August 31, 2023 SUPERSEDED

Anjali Sud

Chief Executive Officer

Anjali Sud was appointed CEO in July 2017 after previously serving as General Manager and Head of Marketing. During her tenure, she oversaw Vimeo's transition to a B2B SaaS video model and its spin-off from IAC into an independent public entity. She stepped down in August 2023.
