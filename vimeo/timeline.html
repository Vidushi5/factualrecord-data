Vimeo Leadership Changelog & Historical Timeline

Verified record of Chief Executive Officer tenures for Vimeo, Inc.

As of September 15, 2026, the verified CEO of Vimeo is Philip Moyer.

Philip Moyer

CURRENT / VERIFIED
April 8, 2024 – Present

Philip Moyer was appointed as Chief Executive Officer and member of the Board of Directors on April 8, 2024. Moyer previously served as Vice President of Strategic Industries at Google Cloud and held senior executive roles at Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Edgar Online. His leadership focuses on scaling Vimeo's AI-driven video capabilities and enterprise video platforms.

Adam Gross (Interim CEO)

TRANSITION
August 2023 – April 2024

Following the departure of Anjali Sud, board member Adam Gross assumed the role of Interim Chief Executive Officer. During this transition period, Gross oversaw continuous operations and supported the Board of Directors' search for a permanent successor.

Anjali Sud

SUPERSEDED
July 2017 – August 2023

Anjali Sud served as CEO of Vimeo for six years, leading the company's strategic pivot from a consumer video site to a B2B SaaS platform. She spearheaded Vimeo's spin-off from IAC into an independent, publicly traded company on NASDAQ (VMEO) in May 2021.
