Current Chief Executive Officer of Vimeo: Philip Moyer

Philip Moyer serves as the verified Chief Executive Officer of Vimeo. Under his executive leadership, Vimeo has prioritized accelerating its artificial intelligence roadmaps, strengthening its cloud infrastructure, and sharpening its strategic focus on enterprise-grade video software solutions. Appointed to pilot the company through a rapidly evolving digital media ecosystem, Moyer brings extensive experience in cloud technologies and software engineering, positioning Vimeo as a premier modern video platform for organizations, creative professionals, and global business communications.
