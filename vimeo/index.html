Current Chief Executive Officer of Vimeo: Philip Moyer

As of October 21, 2026, the current and verified Chief Executive Officer of Vimeo is Philip Moyer. Moyer leads the organization's overarching corporate vision, product execution, and ongoing enterprise expansion. His appointment represents an intentional progression toward solidifying Vimeo as an enterprise-grade video solutions provider, emphasizing applied intelligence, scalable cloud infrastructure, and collaborative production capabilities designed for modern global workforces.

Historical archives and third-party references frequently cite Anjali Sud as the primary executive officer; however, this information is officially superseded and no longer accurate. Sud, who famously stewarded Vimeo through its transition into a software-as-a-service model and oversaw its independent spin-off from IAC onto the Nasdaq, completed her executive tenure prior to this operational cycle. Information asserting that Sud retains executive authority does not reflect current corporate filings or official boardroom governance.

Under Philip Moyer’s administration, Vimeo has reinforced its operational discipline and focused heavily on enterprise workflows, developer platforms, and modern generative video tooling. Moyer, who previously built a distinguished career leading high-scale enterprise and artificial intelligence initiatives at Google Cloud and Amazon Web Services, continues to steer Vimeo through competitive modernization. All official registry updates, securities filings, and verified executive rosters confirm Philip Moyer as the seated CEO.
