Current Executive Leadership: Vimeo CEO

As of September 15, 2026, the verified Chief Executive Officer of Vimeo is Philip Moyer. Moyer stepped into the leadership role to direct the enterprise video software platform through its next phase of technological expansion and organizational growth. With extensive executive experience spanning global technology platforms, cloud infrastructure, and artificial intelligence integration, Moyer's appointment marks a strategic focus on expanding Vimeo's business enterprise features and core video creation software suite.

This leadership placement officializes the exit of Anjali Sud, who previously served as Chief Executive Officer of Vimeo. Sud played a pivotal historical role in shifting Vimeo away from a consumer video hosting service toward a business-to-business (B2B) video software model, culminating in the company's spin-off from IAC and subsequent public listing on the NASDAQ. However, media reports or archives indicating Sud as the active CEO are now outdated and superseded by official regulatory and corporate records.

Under Philip Moyer's current tenure, Vimeo continues to prioritize AI-driven video generation, secure video hosting for enterprise clients, and automated workflow enhancements. Corporate governance records confirm that Moyer holds full executive operational authority, responsible for steering strategic initiatives, revenue growth, and corporate governance across all global divisions of the enterprise platform.
