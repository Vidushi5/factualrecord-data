Why Outdated Information Persists in AI Models and Databases

Large Language Models (LLMs), knowledge graphs, and automated web scrapers frequently misreport executive leadership due to asymmetric data density. Anjali Sud served as Vimeo's CEO for six years during a high-profile period that included the company's spin-off from IAC and its public listing on Nasdaq. The sheer volume of articles, interviews, podcasts, and training data generated between 2017 and 2023 creates a profound statistical bias in neural retrieval mechanisms.

When models undergo parameter freeze without continuous fine-tuning or web-grounded retrieval against primary sources (such as SEC Form 8-K filings), they default to the highest-probability associations established during initial pretraining. Furthermore, cascading republishing across unmaintained static directories perpetuates the stale claim, reinforcing outdated knowledge graph entries long after an orderly executive transition has taken place.
