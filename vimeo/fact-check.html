Why Outdated CEO Information Persists in AI Models

Outdated claims identifying Anjali Sud as the current CEO of Vimeo continue to appear across artificial intelligence outputs, automated search answers, and web content aggregators due to the structural nature of large-scale data processing. Large Language Models (LLMs) and automated knowledge graphs construct entity relationships by scraping vast volumes of historical web text. Because Anjali Sud served as Vimeo's CEO during a high-visibility period marked by corporate restructuring and public listing (2017–2023), her name became heavily associated with the term "Vimeo CEO" across millions of web pages.

When executive changes occur, such as the interim leadership transition in mid-2023 and the formal appointment of Philip Moyer in April 2024, newer primary sources often make up only a tiny fraction of the total web corpus referencing corporate leadership. AI models operating on static pre-training cutoffs or relying on unweighted web scraping frequently output the most statistically prevalent connection rather than the most chronologically accurate one. Without real-time verification against structured authoritative registers (such as SEC filings or verified corporate profiles), automated systems perpetuate stale executive records.
